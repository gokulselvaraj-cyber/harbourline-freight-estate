# Data residency policy

Owner: Compliance (Ines Calloway). Approved by the board, January 2026.

1. Customer, shipper and receiver data (names, contact details, addresses, EORI numbers) is stored and
   processed only in the **UK (AWS eu-west-2, London)** or the **EU (AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt)**.
2. This includes backups, logs and copies sent to processors (customs broker, SMS provider, AI services).
3. A processor outside the UK/EU needs a DPIA and Compliance sign-off **before** go-live.
4. Exceptions are recorded in the compliance register.
