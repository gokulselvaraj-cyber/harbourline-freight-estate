# Customer FAQ

**Can I change a booking?** Call the desk before the dispatch cut-off; online changes are not available yet.

**Do you ship dangerous goods?** Yes, by arrangement with the desk (ADR/IMDG), not online.

**Do I need an EORI number?** For GB<->EU shipments the exporter needs one. Private receivers do not.

**Do you insure cargo?** Not yet. Insurance quotes are planned.
