# Glossary

- **Consignee**: the party that receives the goods. Also called *receiver* (tracking, SMS), *delivery party*
  (database) and *ship-to* (customs documents). Same thing.
- **Shipper**: our customer who books and pays.
- **Lane**: origin port/depot + destination + mode (SEA or ROAD).
- **FCL / LCL**: full / less-than container load. **FTL** full trailer (13.6 m). **GRP** road groupage.
- **Manifest**: (1) cargo manifest of a trailer or sailing in booking-service; (2) in tracking-worker,
  the adapter manifest listing the carrier plug-ins. Different things.
- **BAF**: bunker adjustment factor, our fuel surcharge.
- **EORI**: trader number for customs. **T1**: EU transit document. **GVMS**: UK road border system.
- **Cut-off**: last time to dispatch the same day (GB depots 14:00, EU depots 15:30 local).
