# Rate card rules (2026)

Owner: Pricing (with the booking squad). Rates themselves are in the lane tariff (`lane_tariff` table).

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Road groupage chargeable weight | greater of actual kg and volume m3 x **333** (1:3), rounded up to the kg |
| Sea LCL | W/M: greater of tonnes and m3 |
| Fuel surcharge (BAF) | % of base freight, reviewed quarterly; see the customer rate card workbook |
| Customs documents fee | GBP 45.00 per GB<->EU booking |
| Dangerous goods (ADR/IMDG) | refer to the desk, not bookable online |
| Weight bands | 1: <=500 kg, 2: <=2 t, 3: <=5 t, 4: <=12 t, 5: >12 t |

Quarterly revisions: 1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul 2026 (+3.5% and +6% on Q1). Norway lanes from 1 Sep 2026.
