How to book a shipment

1. Choose your lane

Pick origin, destination and mode (sea or road). Only lanes we serve are listed.

2. Describe the cargo

Enter packages, gross weight and volume for each line. Add the HS code if you know it; otherwise we classify it for you.

3. Your price

The price is the lane rate plus a fuel surcharge of 11.5% and, for shipments between Great Britain and the EU, a customs documents fee of £45.00.

4. Dispatch

Bookings confirmed before 14:00 (UK depots) or 15:30 (EU depots) can leave the same working day.

5. Tracking

The receiver gets a text message with the expected delivery time, and another one if it changes.
